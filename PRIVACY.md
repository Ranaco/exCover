# exCover privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

exCover is made by Rana ("I", "me"). This policy explains what exCover does with your data.
The short version: exCover has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and I never
receive any of your data.

## What stays on your phone

- **GIFs you import.** GIFs you pick from Photos, download from GIPHY or import from a link are
  copied into exCover's private storage on your phone so they can play on your screens. They
  never leave your phone, unless you choose to save one back to Photos.
- **Your settings.** Which GIF goes on which screen, your framing, favourites, clock theme and
  always-on display look are stored on your phone only.

Uninstalling exCover deletes all of this.

## What goes over the internet

exCover connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **GIPHY search.** When you search GIPHY inside exCover, your search words are sent to GIPHY's
  servers to get results, and the GIFs you open are downloaded from GIPHY. GIPHY handles this
  under its own privacy policy: https://support.giphy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032872931
- **GIF links.** When you paste a link and import it, exCover downloads the GIF from that address.
  The website you link to sees an ordinary download request from your phone.

exCover sends nothing else, and nothing at all to me.

## Children

exCover isn't directed at children under 13.
